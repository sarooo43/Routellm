Design Patterns Used (mention these in your resume — recruiters love this):

Strategy Pattern → each provider is a swappable ModelProvider implementation
Circuit Breaker Pattern → resilience engineering
Chain of Responsibility → fallback execution chain
Observer/Event Logging → every routing decision emits a log event
